Common questions

Where are my notes stored?

In the app’s private storage on your iPhone. There is no account or developer-hosted copy. Deleting the app removes its local data.

How do I move my notes to another iPhone?

In Settings & backup, choose “Back up text data.” Keep the copied JSON somewhere private. On the other device, copy that JSON to the clipboard and choose “Restore from backup.” Restoring replaces the notebook on that device. Photos are not included and must be added again.

How do I try the app without real information?

When your notebook is empty, choose “Try search with sample people” in Settings & backup. It adds 7 fictional people. Samples cannot be mixed into a notebook that already contains people or entries.

Can I change the language?

The English-enabled version follows your preferred supported device language: English or Japanese. You can also use iOS’s app language setting. Changing the language does not translate or change your notes.
